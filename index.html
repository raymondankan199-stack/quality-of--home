Privacy Policy

Quality of Home · Accra, Ghana · Last updated: August 2026

1. Who we are

Quality of Home (“we”, “us”) operates an online store selling products for delivery in Ghana. This notice explains what personal information we collect and how we use it when you use our website or app.

2. Information we collect

We may collect and store:

  • Identity & contact: name, phone number, email (if you provide it)
  • Delivery details: address, area, and location you choose at checkout
  • Order data: products ordered, amounts, payment method, order status
  • Trade-in details: device description and notes you submit
  • Technical data: basic device/browser information needed to run the site
  • Chat messages: questions you send to the in-app assistant

Payment card and Mobile Money secrets are processed by Paystack. We do not store your full card number or MoMo PIN on our servers.

3. How we use your information

  • To create and manage your account
  • To process and deliver your orders
  • To confirm payments and prevent fraud
  • To respond to trade-in requests and support messages
  • To improve our products, prices, and customer experience
  • To contact you about your order via phone or WhatsApp when needed
  • To meet legal or regulatory obligations in Ghana

4. How we share information

We only share what is needed to run the service:

  • Paystack — to process payments securely
  • Delivery partners — name, phone, and address to deliver your order
  • Service providers — hosting and database used to store order data
  • Authorities — if required by law

We do not sell your personal information to other companies for their marketing.

5. WhatsApp

If you choose to contact us on WhatsApp, that chat is also subject to WhatsApp’s own terms and privacy policy. We use WhatsApp to confirm orders and provide support.

6. Data security

We take reasonable technical and organisational steps to protect your data. No online system is 100% secure, but we work to keep access limited and payments handled by trusted providers.

7. How long we keep data

We keep order and customer records as long as needed for delivery, support, accounting, and legal requirements. You may ask us to update or remove information where the law allows.

8. Your rights

Subject to Ghanaian law, you may request to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Ask us to delete data we no longer need
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent

Contact us using the details below to make a request.

9. Children

Our store is intended for adults. If you believe a child has provided personal data, please contact us so we can remove it.

10. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do.
